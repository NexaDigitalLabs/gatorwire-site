Privacy policy

Last updated November 10, 2026.

Who we are

Gator Wire is a fan-run GTA 6 news page. This policy covers our website and the app we use to publish to our own YouTube, Instagram and TikTok accounts.

What our app accesses

Our app signs in only to Gator Wire's own accounts on YouTube, Instagram and TikTok, with the permissions needed to upload our videos and posts and to read their view and comment counts. It does not sign in to anyone else's account.

What we collect from visitors and viewers

We do not collect, sell or share personal information from people who visit this site or watch our posts. This site uses no cookies, analytics or ads. Public comments on our posts stay on the platform where they were posted, under that platform's own privacy policy.

How we store account access

The sign-in keys for our own accounts are stored privately on our own computers and are never shared or sold.

Google user data

Our use of information received from Google APIs follows the Google API Services User Data Policy, including its Limited Use requirements. We use YouTube access only to upload and manage Gator Wire's own videos. YouTube is covered by the YouTube Terms of Service and the Google Privacy Policy.

Deleting data

To ask about or delete any information, email gatorwire.studio@gmail.com. We answer within 30 days. You can also remove our app's access to a Google account at myaccount.google.com/permissions.

Changes

If this policy changes, the new version and its date appear on this page.
