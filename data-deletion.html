Data deletion

Last updated November 10, 2026.

Gator Wire's app signs in only to Gator Wire's own accounts and does not collect personal information from other people. If you believe we hold any information about you and want it deleted:

How to ask

Email gatorwire.studio@gmail.com with the subject "Data deletion" and the account name or details it concerns. We delete it and confirm by email within 30 days.

Removing app access yourself

Facebook and Instagram: Settings, then Apps and websites, then remove Gator Wire. Google and YouTube: myaccount.google.com/permissions. TikTok: Settings, then Security and permissions, then Apps and services permissions.
